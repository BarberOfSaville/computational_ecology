~COMPUTATIONAL ECOLOGY REPOSITORY~

I am a PhD student at Fordham University in New York City. My dissertation research studies bee communities in urban gardens, and I aim to explore how local habitat factors, connectivity across a network of sites, and functional ecology traits interact to inform the bee communities in our urban matrix. 

During my PhD studies, I aim to build my computational skills, specifically in statistics, data processing, and spatial analysis. I aim to pursue a career that puts these skills to use toward conservation goals.
